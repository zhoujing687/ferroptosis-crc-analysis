Interpretation and provenance

These strings reproduce the displayed Boolean expressions. In Fig 1, the WoS expression does not contain its year filter, whereas the Scopus expression does. The figure lists English language and Article/Review eligibility separately. The archived materials do not establish whether each restriction was applied using an interface filter or during subsequent screening. This transcription therefore does not add guessed filter syntax or claim an exact rerun of the historical interface session.

The ferroptosis-related domain uses OR alternatives that include ROS, iron metabolism, and lipid peroxidation. A record can meet this domain without explicitly mentioning ferroptosis. Topic relevance was screened as described in Methods. The exported counts were 472 WoSCC and 624 Scopus records; 326 combined exclusions yielded 770 retained documents (518 articles and 252 reviews). The archive has no per-reason exclusion ledger. Subscription entitlements, index updates, and later citation accrual can change current retrieval results.
